To round out the Clinical Precision design system for MedPrecision, here are the stylistic and structural guidelines used across the interface:

Visual Style & Depth
Shadow Style: Soft & Layered – We use subtle, multi-layered shadows (similar to Tailwind's shadow-sm and shadow) to create depth without visual clutter. This ensures that cards and modals feel like they are floating slightly above the light-tinted background.
Border Style: Visible & Refined – The interface uses light, 1px borders (#E2E8F0) for all primary containers, inputs, and dividers. This provides clear structural definition and a "technical" feel that reinforces the clinical theme.
Iconography & Density
Icon Style: Outlined (1.5px Stroke) – Following the Lucide/Shadcn standard, icons are clean and geometric with a 1.5px stroke weight. They are consistently sized at 20px for UI actions and 24px for section headers to maintain a balanced visual weight.
Density: Spacious – The design prioritizes legibility and reduced cognitive load. With 24px standard padding and 32px section gaps, the layout feels "breathable" and premium, ensuring critical medical data is easy to scan.
These final specifications ensure that the MedPrecision SaaS platform feels cohesive, modern, and highly professional.
