Based on the Clinical Precision design system, here are the typographic specifications used throughout the MedPrecision SaaS platform:

Font Families
Sans (UI): Manrope – A modern, geometric sans-serif that balances clinical professionalism with SaaS-style approachability.
Arabic-friendly (RTL): IBM Plex Sans Arabic – This is the recommended companion for Manrope to maintain the same technical and modern feel in RTL layouts.
Typography Scale
Base size: 14px (0.875rem) – Used for standard body text and UI labels to maximize information density while maintaining legibility.
Heading Scale:
H1: 36px (2.25rem) – Primary page titles (e.g., Dashboard Overview).
H2: 24px (1.5rem) – Section headers and card titles.
H3: 18px (1.125rem) – Subsection headers.
H4: 16px (1rem) – Small labels or group headers.
Hierarchy & Rhythm
Line height: 1.5 (21px for base text) – Provides optimal breathing room for medical records and long-form data.
Letter spacing: -0.02em for headings – Tightened slightly for a more "premium" and intentional look in larger sizes.
Font Weights:
400 (Regular): Body copy, descriptions, and secondary data.
500 (Medium): Button text, navigation items, and table headers.
600 (Semi-bold): Sub-titles and emphasized labels.
700 (Bold): Page headings and primary brand elements.
These settings ensure that the interface remains readable under "clinical" conditions while maintaining a sleek, modern aesthetic.
